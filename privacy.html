Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Application: AP Spanish Trainer (ap-spanish-trainer.netlify.app)

1. Zero Server Storage & Zero Data Collection

AP Spanish Trainer is a 100% client-side static web application. We do not operate a backend database, and we do not collect, store, read, sell, or transmit your personal information, study history, or custom flashcards to our servers or to any third party.

2. Local Browser Storage

By default, all spaced-repetition review intervals, daily study counts, custom Preply phrases, and optional audio preferences are saved exclusively inside your own web browser's localStorage on your device.

3. Google Sign-In & Google Drive AppData Usage

If you optionally choose to click "Sign in with Google" to sync your progress across devices, the application requests permission solely for the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: Allows the app to create and update only its own hidden application configuration files (ap-spanish-progress-v2.json and its automatic backup) inside your personal Google Drive's isolated AppData folder. The app cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your other Google Drive files, folders, or documents.
  • userinfo.email & userinfo.profile: Used strictly to display your name/email in the top navigation bar while you are signed in. This information stays in your browser session and is never sent to any external server.

Your newly added phrases and spaced-repetition progress are stored directly in your own Google Drive under your full ownership and control. You may revoke access or delete the app's hidden data at any time through your Google Account Permissions or Google Drive settings ("Manage Apps").

4. Optional Text-to-Speech Services

Audio playback uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis by default. If you voluntarily enter your own Google Cloud Text-to-Speech API key in Audio Settings, that key is stored solely in your browser's localStorage and sent directly from your browser to Google's Text-to-Speech API when you play a sentence.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

AP Spanish Trainer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the site maintainer via the project's GitHub repository: github.com/sabujp/ap-spanish-trainer.
