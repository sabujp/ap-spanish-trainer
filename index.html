How to Use AP Spanish Trainer

A 10–15 minute daily system built for the AP Spanish Language & Culture exam.

1. Daily Spaced Repetition (20 New Chunks/Day)

Instead of memorizing 50 isolated words, study full AP-level sentences with the key phrase blanked out. Rate each card honestly so the SM-2 algorithm schedules reviews right before you forget them:

  • 1. Again (Forgot): Resets progress and shows the card again in 1 minute.
  • 2. Hard (Struggled): Tightens the card's ease multiplier and brings it back sooner (12 hours or 1.2× current interval).
  • 3. Good (Recalled): Standard spacing (1 day → 3 days → interval × ease).
  • 4. Easy (Effortless): Increases the card's ease multiplier and pushes it out further (4 days or 1.3× boost).

2. Capture "Gap Phrases" from Preply Tutors

During sessions with his Argentine or Bolivian tutors, whenever he gets stuck expressing an idea, have the tutor type the natural Spanish sentence in chat. Paste those lines into Tab 2 (Preply Quick-Add) right after class.

3. Practice the 2-Minute AP Cultural Comparison

Use Tab 3 twice a week. It simulates AP Speaking Task 4 (4 minutes prep, 2 minutes speaking) and challenges him to weave 3 learned transition chunks into a comparison between his community and Argentina/Bolivia.

4. Natural Google Voices & Google Drive Sync

When hosted over https:// on Netlify, Chrome automatically unlocks natural Google español voices (or add a free-tier Google Cloud TTS key in Audio Settings for offline/iOS). Click Sign in with Google in the top bar so every card rating and custom phrase automatically backs up to your personal Google Drive.
