How to Use AP Spanish Trainer

A 10–15 minute daily system built for the AP Spanish Language & Culture exam.

1. Three Study Modes Under Tab 1 (Study)

  • 1) Chunk / Phrase Tests (SM-2 SRS): Study full AP sentences (20 new chunks/day by default) with the key phrase blanked out. Enable Word Lookup when you want instant English meanings by hovering or highlighting any Spanish word (Off by default). Rate recall 1–4: Again (1 min), Hard (12h / 1.2×), Good (1d → 3d), Easy (4d / 1.3× boost).
  • 2) Vocab Test (Typed Only): Practice forward (ES → EN), backward (EN → ES), or mixed typed recall on words & AP phrases—prioritizing the exact words and phrases from your Phrase Tests at your selected Level.
  • 3) Conjugation Test (Full Matrix w/ vosotros): Drills all 414 verbs from the phrase packs in two steps: type the Spanish infinitive from English, then complete all 6 persons including vosotros across 8 AP tenses—prioritizing verbs used in your Phrase Tests at your selected Level.

2. Capture "Gap Phrases" from Preply Tutors

During sessions with his Argentine or Bolivian tutors, whenever he gets stuck expressing an idea, have the tutor type the natural Spanish sentence in chat. Paste those lines into Tab 2 (Preply Quick-Add) right after class.

3. Practice the 2-Minute AP Cultural Comparison

Use Tab 3 twice a week. It simulates AP Speaking Task 4 (4 minutes prep, 2 minutes speaking) and challenges him to weave 3 learned transition chunks into a comparison between his community and Argentina/Bolivia.

4. Natural Google Voices & Google Drive Sync

When hosted over https:// on Netlify, Chrome automatically unlocks natural Google español voices (or add a free-tier Google Cloud TTS key in Audio Settings for offline/iOS). Click Sign in with Google in the top bar so every card rating, selected level, and custom phrase automatically backs up to your personal Google Drive.
